what you'll learn

this is a construction course — you'll learn how to build the structure of a face correctly from any angle. not rendering, not shading — the foundation that makes everything else possible.

  • perspective & tilt: how to capture 3D on a flat page using guidelines — the step most people skip
  • the head shape: proportions, jaw, and the overlay study method that builds real understanding
  • the mask: placing big shapes for eye sockets, nose, and mouth before touching any details
  • eyes: guideline placement, perspective warping, and seeing the eye as a 3D ball
  • nose & lips: simplified sphere construction that works from any angle
